   Step-Down Transformer: Lowers mains AC voltage to a safe, low AC voltage (mounted with top terminal contacts).

AC Power Cord & 2-Pin Plug: Connects the transformer’s primary winding to the mains wall socket.

Rectifier Diodes (4x): Standard silicone rectifier diodes (typically 1N400x series) used to build a full-wave bridge rectifier to convert AC to pulsating DC.

Electrolytic Capacitors (3x): Radial-lead smoothing/filter capacitors to filter out AC ripple and stabilize the voltage.

Voltage Regulator IC with Heatsink: A 3-pin linear regulator in a TO-220 package (typically LM7805 or LM7812) fitted with a black aluminum heatsink for thermal dissipation.

USB-A Female Port: Connector to provide a standard 5V output for USB-powered devices or test leads.

Solderless Breadboard: For prototyping the circuit without soldering (has a small component, likely a switch or crystal, resting on top).


3. We asked AI about how to test the diodes and LM 7805.

Turn the dial to the Resistance ($\Omega$) mode (or Continuity mode with the speaker/diode symbol).
Test the key pin combinations using both polarities of the probes (switching red and black)
Should show a very high resistance or an "OL" (Over Limit / Open) reading.
